👑 Xavi Hernández

Xavi Hernández

Nationality: 🇪🇸 Spain

Position: Midfielder

Barcelona Jersey: #6

Barcelona Career: 1998–2015

Xavi Hernández is one of FC Barcelona's greatest legends. Known for his incredible passing, vision, intelligence and control, he was the heart of Barcelona's legendary midfield era.

📰 Legacy

Xavi was the leader of Barcelona's famous possession-based football style. His passing, composure and understanding of the game made him one of the best midfielders ever.
