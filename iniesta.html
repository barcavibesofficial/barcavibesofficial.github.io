Andrés Iniesta

Nationality: 🇪🇸 Spain

Position: Midfielder

Andrés Iniesta is one of FC Barcelona's greatest legends. Known for his elegance, vision, passing and incredible football intelligence, he was a key player during Barcelona's most successful era.
